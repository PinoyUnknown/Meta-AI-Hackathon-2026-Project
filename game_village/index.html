VICTORY CONDITIONS:

1. Conquest Victory: Build an army of Swordsmen, Archers, and Knights to destroy the Rival Red Civilization's Town Center and Castle across the river.

2. Wonder Victory: Reach the Imperial Age, build a Wonder of the World, and defend it for 200 seconds against enemy onslaughts!

THE FOUR AGES PROGRESSION:

• Dark Age: Gather Food and Wood. Build Houses to support 20 population. Train Villagers and Militia.

• Feudal Age (500 Food): Unlocks Barracks, Archery Range, Stable, Blacksmith, Watchtower, and Stone Walls. Train Archers and Scouts.

• Castle Age (800 Food, 200 Gold): Unlocks Castles, Monasteries, Knights, Crossbowmen, and Monks. Monks can pick up Relics for gold and convert enemies ("WOLOLO")!

• Imperial Age (1000 Food, 800 Gold): Unlocks Paladins, Champions, Trebuchets, and the Wonder of the World.

RTS CONTROLS & HOTKEYS:

• Box Selection: Left-click and drag a green box to select multiple units.

• Smart Right-Click: Right-click terrain to move, right-click resources to gather, right-click enemies to attack, right-click buildings to repair or garrison.

• Z or .: Cycle through idle villagers. B: Ring Town Bell.

• WASD / Arrow Keys: Pan camera. Mouse Wheel: Zoom.

• F5 / F9: Quick-Save / Quick-Load.

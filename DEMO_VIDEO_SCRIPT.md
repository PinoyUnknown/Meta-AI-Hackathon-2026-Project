## 🎥 Recording Setup & Quick Tips (Windows 10)
- **Screen Recorder**: Use **OBS Studio** (Recommended: 1080p 60fps) or **Windows Game Bar** (`Win + Alt + R`).
- **Audio**: Use a clear USB microphone or headset. Ensure background noise suppression is enabled.
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (16:9).
- **Preparation Before Hitting Record**:
  1. Open a PowerShell terminal and run: `python main.py` (Desktop GUI window opens).
  2. Have your browser open in the background with `http://localhost:8000` (run `python main.py --web` in a second tab).
  3. Keep this script on a second monitor or phone so you can speak smoothly.

METAHORIZON ODYSSEY: NOVA GALAXY 3D is an original 3D high-velocity space flight and tactical fleet combat simulation built natively with Three.js WebGL and procedural Web Audio synthesis. Explore a panoramic, draggable 3D galaxy inspired by the acclaimed Korean galaxy game NOVA, complete with dynamic comets, orbital satellites, volumetric cosmic fogs, and fierce combat against supersonic jet interceptors, heavy rocket gunships, and relentless alien bio-swarms.

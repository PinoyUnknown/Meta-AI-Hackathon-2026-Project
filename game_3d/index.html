[STRATEGY GUIDE & REALM HINTS]

1. Open-World Geography & Biomes

Your realm is set within a massive 800m open world. To the South lie fertile meadows; to the East lies the Ancient Deep Jungle rich in wild boars and berry thickets; to the North rise towering Rocky Mountains feeding an alpine lake and rushing waterfalls; to the West across the river gorge lies the hostile Crimson Wolf Clan encampment.

2. Worker Vocations & Direct Resource Looting

Click any villager to open their Inspector Card and reassign them to 10 vocations: Woodcutter, Stone Mason, Gold Miner, Forager, Fisherman, Farmer, Baker, Blacksmith, Royal Guard, or Hunter! You can also left-click directly on any tree, granite boulder, gold quartz vein, berry bush, or wild animal in the world to immediately dispatch nearest workers to loot it!

3. Hunting Wild Game in the Jungle

Assign Hunters to track and hunt wild boars in the dense eastern jungle, stags in the forest edges, and wolves near mountain ridges. Hunting yields fresh meat and leather hides to sustain your settlers and satisfy royal bounties.

4. Defense Against Rival Enemy Tribes

The Crimson Wolf Marauder Clan periodically sends raiding parties across the river gorge. Construct the Stone Gatehouse and Wall battlements, and assign Royal Guards to patrol borders and repel raiders for large gold and prestige bounties!

5. Dynamic Weather & Production Chains

Experience clear sunshine, heavy rainstorms, rolling mountain mist, and golden sunsets. Windmills grind grain, Bakeries bake warm bread, Blacksmiths forge iron weapons, and Fishing Docks harvest the river.
